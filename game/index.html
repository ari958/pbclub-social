How to play

Three shots. One AI coach.

  • TIMING

    The AI moves your feet. You swing. Hit when the ring around the ball closes. Early pulls it crosscourt, late pushes it down the line.

  • DINK

    Soft into their kitchen. Use it on low balls and to get to the net. Patience wins dink rallies.

  • DRIVE

    Hard and flat. Use it on high balls and returns. Drive a low ball and it ends up in the net.

  • LOB

    Over their heads. Use it when both robots crowd the kitchen line. Mistime it and you get smashed.

  • AIM

    Tap the robots' side of the court, or hold ← →. Tap anywhere else (or press Space) and the AI picks the shot. Of course it does.

  • VOLLEY

    At the kitchen line you'll hit some balls out of the air. Never from inside the kitchen. The AI is watching.
